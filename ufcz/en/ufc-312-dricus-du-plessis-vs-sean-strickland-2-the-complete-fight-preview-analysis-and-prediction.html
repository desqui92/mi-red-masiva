UFC 312: Dricus du Plessis vs Sean Strickland 2 – The Complete Fight Preview, Analysis, and Prediction

Introduction: The Rematch That Will Define a Division

The UFC middleweight division is set for its most anticipated rematch of the year as reigning champion Dricus du Plessis prepares to defend his title against former champion Sean Strickland at UFC 312. This isn't just another title fight; it's a collision of two distinct fighting philosophies, two contrasting personalities, and two men who genuinely dislike each other. Their first encounter at UFC 297 was a razor-thin split decision that left fans divided and the division in a state of uncertainty. Now, with the stakes higher than ever, the question on everyone's mind is simple: Can Strickland solve the puzzle that du Plessis presents, or will "Stillknocks" prove that his victory was no fluke?

In this comprehensive guide, we will break down every aspect of UFC 312, from the fighters' styles and recent form to the tactical keys to victory, the full fight card, and our final prediction. Whether you're a hardcore MMA fan or a casual observer looking to get up to speed, this article has everything you need to know about one of the biggest fights of the year.

Fight Details: When and Where to Watch UFC 312

  • Event: UFC 312: Du Plessis vs. Strickland 2
  • Date: February 8, 2025
  • Location: Qudos Bank Arena, Sydney, Australia
  • Main Card Start Time: 10:00 PM ET (7:00 PM PT) / 2:00 PM AEDT (Feb 9)
  • Broadcast: ESPN+ Pay-Per-View (US), TNT Sports Box Office (UK), Main Event (Australia)

For the second consecutive time, du Plessis will headline a card in Australia, a country that has embraced the South African champion as one of their own. The atmosphere in Sydney is expected to be electric, with a raucous crowd likely to be firmly behind the champion.

The Stakes: Why This Fight Matters

Beyond the obvious gold on the line, this rematch carries significant implications for the future of the middleweight division. A win for du Plessis solidifies his reign and sets up potential super-fights with the likes of Khamzat Chimaev or a move to light heavyweight. A win for Strickland, on the other hand, would be one of the most remarkable redemption arcs in recent UFC history, proving that his controversial loss was a mere hiccup in an otherwise dominant run.

"I don't fight for legacy, I fight for money and to hurt people," Strickland has stated repeatedly. Du Plessis, conversely, fights for the glory of his nation and to prove he is the best in the world. This clash of motivations adds an extra layer of intrigue to an already compelling matchup.

Fighter Profile: Dricus du Plessis – The Unorthodox Champion

Record and Background

  • Record: 22-2-0 (14 KOs, 10 Submissions)
  • Team: Team CIT / Dricus Du Plessis MMA
  • Age: 31
  • Height: 6'1" (185 cm)
  • Reach: 76" (193 cm)

Style and Strengths

Du Plessis is a paradox. He is not the most polished striker, nor is he the most technical grappler, yet he finds a way to win. His style is chaotic, aggressive, and incredibly powerful. He thrives in the chaos of a fight, often absorbing damage to deliver even more devastating shots. His wrestling background (he was a two-time South African national champion) gives him a solid base, but it's his raw power and relentless pace that often overwhelm opponents.

  • Power: He possesses fight-ending power in both hands. His knockout of Robert Whittaker and his TKO of Darren Till are testaments to his devastating finishing ability.
  • Grappling: While not a traditional wrestler, his top pressure and ground-and-pound are relentless. He uses his strength to smother opponents and grind them down.
  • Cardio: His pace is relentless. He pushes forward for all five rounds, making him a nightmare to fight against.
  • Mentality: Unshakeable confidence. He believes he is destined for greatness, and this belief often carries him through tough moments.

Fighter Profile: Sean Strickland – The Unfiltered Contender

Record and Background

  • Record: 29-6-0 (11 KOs, 4 Submissions)
  • Team: Xtreme Couture
  • Age: 33
  • Height: 6'1" (185 cm)
  • Reach: 76" (193 cm)

Style and Strengths

Strickland is the antithesis of du Plessis. Where the champion is chaotic, Strickland is methodical. He is a pressure fighter who uses a high-volume jab and relentless forward movement to break opponents down. His defense is underrated; he uses a high guard and head movement to slip punches, making him difficult to hit cleanly. He is the definition of a volume puncher, and his cardio is arguably the best in the division.

  • Jab: The most important weapon in his arsenal. He throws it with incredible volume and accuracy, setting up his power shots.
  • Defense: His high guard and upper-body movement are excellent. He rarely gets hit cleanly.
  • Durability: He has never been knocked out in his professional career. His chin is granite.
  • Fight IQ: He sticks to his game plan with discipline. He rarely gets drawn into brawls, preferring to out-point his opponents.

Tactical Breakdown: The Keys to Victory

To understand how this fight might play out, we need to analyze the tactical keys for both men. The first fight was a close, competitive affair that could have gone either way. What needs to change for each fighter to secure a definitive win this time?

Keys to Victory for Dricus du Plessis

  • Close the Distance: Du Plessis cannot afford to fight at range against Strickland. He must close the distance quickly and make it a dirty, ugly fight. He needs to turn this into a brawl, not a boxing match.
  • Target the Body: Strickland's high guard leaves his body exposed. Du Plessis should attack the liver and ribs with kicks and punches to slow down his opponent's movement and lower his guard.
  • Use Wrestling: While he won't out-wrestle Strickland, he can use takedown attempts to disrupt his rhythm. Even if he doesn't complete the takedown, the threat of it will keep Strickland guessing.
  • Explosive Entries: Du Plessis needs to use his explosive power to close the distance in bursts, rather than walking forward into Strickland's jab.

Keys to Victory for Sean Strickland

  • Maintain Distance: Strickland's jab is his best friend. He must keep du Plessis at the end of his range and not allow him to get inside. The longer the fight stays in the pocket, the more it favors Strickland.
  • Volume, Volume, Volume: He needs to throw more punches than in the first fight. He landed at a decent clip, but he needs to increase the output to out-volume the champion.
  • Check the Kicks: Du Plessis will likely throw leg kicks. Strickland must check them to negate their impact and avoid compromising his own movement.
  • Stay Disciplined: The worst thing Strickland can do is get drawn into a brawl. He must stick to his game plan and not get frustrated by du Plessis's unorthodox movement.

Comparing the First Fight: What Went Wrong (and Right) for Each Fighter

Let's rewind to UFC 297 in January 2024. The fight was a tactical battle, with Strickland controlling the first two rounds with his jab. However, du Plessis began to find his range in the third round, landing powerful hooks and kicks. The final two rounds were extremely close, with both men having their moments. In the end, two judges scored it for du Plessis, while one scored it for Strickland.

For Strickland, the key takeaway was that he won the early rounds but faded slightly in the championship rounds. He needs to pace himself better and increase his output in the later rounds. For du Plessis, the lesson was that he cannot afford to give away early rounds. He needs to start faster and establish his offense earlier in the fight.

Prediction and Final Verdict

This is an incredibly difficult fight to call. The first fight was razor-thin, and both men have likely made adjustments. However, there are a few factors that lean slightly in favor of the champion.

Dricus du Plessis has shown a remarkable ability to evolve and adapt mid-fight. He is also fighting in front of a crowd that will be firmly behind him, which provides a psychological boost. His power is the great equalizer; one clean shot can change the course of the fight.

Sean Strickland, on the other hand, is the more consistent and disciplined fighter. He is a master of his game plan, and if he can execute it perfectly for 25 minutes, he will likely win a decision. However, his style often leaves fights in the hands of the judges, and that can be a dangerous gamble.

The Verdict: Expect another closely contested battle, but this time, du Plessis will learn from his early struggles. He will start faster and land the more significant shots. In a fight that could go either way, the champion's power and the home crowd advantage will be the deciding factors. Prediction: Dricus du Plessis wins via TKO in Round 4.

Conclusion: A Night of Redemption and Glory

UFC 312 promises to be a spectacular event. The main event is a genuine 50/50 fight between two of the most compelling characters in the sport. Whether you love the chaotic power of du Plessis or the unfiltered honesty of Strickland, this is a fight that demands your attention. With a stacked card from top to bottom, the Qudos Bank Arena is set to witness a historic night of mixed martial arts. Don't miss it.
