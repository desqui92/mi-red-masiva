UFC 312: Du Plessis vs Strickland 2 – The Complete Breakdown, Fight Card, and Prediction

The UFC returns to Sydney, Australia, for a massive pay-per-view event that promises to settle one of the most heated rivalries in the middleweight division. When Dricus du Plessis and Sean Strickland step into the Octagon for the second time, they will not just be fighting for the belt—they will be fighting for closure. This is your complete guide to UFC 312, covering everything from the main event breakdown, the full fight card, stylistic analysis, and our official prediction.

The Main Event: A Rivalry Built on Bad Blood

The animosity between Dricus du Plessis and Sean Strickland is not manufactured. It is visceral, personal, and deeply rooted in their contrasting philosophies. Their first encounter at UFC 297 was a razor-thin split decision that left the MMA world divided. Now, with the score settled at 1-0 in favor of "Stillknocks," Strickland gets his immediate rematch.

This is not just a title fight; it is a clash of identities. Strickland represents the abrasive, no-nonsense American style, while du Plessis embodies the relentless, unorthodox power of South African MMA. The tension is palpable, and the stakes have never been higher.

Dricus du Plessis: The Unorthodox Champion

Dricus du Plessis (21-2) is a paradox. He is a fighter who looks vulnerable on the feet yet consistently finds a way to win. His striking is unconventional, often leaving his chin exposed, but his physical strength and explosive power are generational. He does not fight pretty; he fights to hurt you.

  • Key Strengths: Raw power, incredible cardio, and a granite chin that absorbs punishment.
  • Finishing Ability: 9 wins by KO/TKO and 11 by submission, proving he is a threat everywhere.
  • X-Factor: His ability to break opponents mentally. He thrives in chaos, and his pressure is suffocating.
  • Weakness: Defensive lapses. He gets hit cleanly too often, which is a dangerous habit against a precision puncher like Strickland.

Sean Strickland: The Precision Sharpshooter

Sean Strickland (28-6) is the exact opposite of his rival. Where du Plessis relies on brute force, Strickland relies on mathematics. He fights behind a high guard with a relentless jab, controlling distance and pace with metronomic consistency. He is the most defensively responsible fighter in the division.

  • Key Strengths: Elite defensive boxing, a laser-like jab, and an unmatched ability to dictate the range.
  • Durability: He has never been knocked out in his professional career.
  • Game Plan: He will look to keep the fight at range, avoid the power shots, and out-point du Plessis over five rounds.
  • Weakness: He lacks one-punch knockout power. He wins by accumulation, not devastation.

Fight Analysis: How Will the Rematch Play Out?

The first fight was a war of attrition. Strickland controlled the early rounds with his jab, but du Plessis stormed back in the later rounds with relentless pressure and takedowns. The rematch hinges on one critical question: Can Strickland maintain his output for 25 minutes, or will du Plessis's pressure eventually break him again?

"The first fight was close, but this time I'm going to put him away. He talks too much for a guy who runs backwards." - Dricus du Plessis

Strickland’s path to victory is clear: keep it standing, stay behind the jab, and never let du Plessis get his rhythm. He must avoid the clinch and the ground at all costs. If he can make it a boxing match, he wins 49-46 on the scorecards.

Du Plessis’s path is equally clear: he must turn it into a brawl. He needs to close the distance, make it ugly, and test Strickland's chin with heavy hooks to the body and head. If he can land one clean overhand right, this fight ends early.

Full UFC 312 Fight Card

While the main event is the main attraction, the rest of the card is stacked with high-level talent. Here is the complete lineup for the night.

Main Card (Pay-Per-View)

  • Middleweight Championship: Dricus du Plessis (c) vs. Sean Strickland
  • Women's Strawweight Championship: Zhang Weili (c) vs. Tatiana Suarez
  • Heavyweight: Tai Tuivasa vs. Alexander Romanov
  • Light Heavyweight: Khalil Rountree Jr. vs. Jamahal Hill
  • Welterweight: Jack Della Maddalena vs. Gilbert Burns

Preliminary Card (ESPN/ESPN+)

  • Middleweight: Jacob Malkoun vs. Rodolfo Vieira
  • Featherweight: Kevin Vallejos vs. Alex Caceres
  • Light Heavyweight: Marcin Prachnio vs. Vitor Petrino
  • Women's Flyweight: Maycee Barber vs. Casey O'Neill

Early Prelims (UFC Fight Pass)

  • Featherweight: Zafir Ahmad vs. Muhammad Naimov
  • Women's Flyweight: Yazmin Jauregui vs. Julia Polastri
  • Middleweight: Kennedy Nzechukwu vs. Karl Williams

Co-Main Event: The Return of Tatiana Suarez

One of the most compelling storylines on this card is the return of Tatiana Suarez. After years of injury layoffs, the undefeated wrestling prodigy finally gets her title shot against the dominant Zhang Weili. This is a classic grappler vs. striker matchup.

Zhang Weili is the most well-rounded strawweight champion in history. Her striking is crisp, her cardio is elite, and her takedown defense has improved drastically. However, Suarez is a different animal. If she gets top position, she is nearly impossible to escape. This fight will determine the future of the division.

Key Factors to Watch

Beyond the main event, there are several factors that could define the night. Here are the key variables to keep an eye on.

  • The Australian Crowd: With Tai Tuivasa and Jack Della Maddalena on the card, the crowd will be electric. Home-field advantage is real in MMA.
  • Khalil Rountree's Return: After his epic war against Alex Pereira, Rountree faces a dangerous Jamahal Hill. This fight could steal the show.
  • The Weight Cut: Du Plessis has struggled with weight cuts in the past. If he looks drained at the weigh-in, Strickland's pressure will be even more effective.

Prediction and Final Verdict

This is an incredibly difficult fight to call. The first bout was decided by a single round, and both fighters have likely made adjustments. However, the physicality of du Plessis is a problem that Strickland cannot solve.

Strickland's style relies on volume and consistency. Du Plessis's style relies on breaking your will. In a five-round fight, the pressure eventually becomes too much. Strickland will win the early rounds, but the tide will turn in the championship rounds.

Official Prediction: Dricus du Plessis wins via TKO in Round 4. The relentless pressure and power will be too much for Strickland to handle, and the South African will cement his status as the future of the middleweight division.

How to Watch UFC 312

For fans in the United States, the event is available on ESPN+ pay-per-view. The prelims will air on ESPN, with early prelims on UFC Fight Pass. Internationally, the event will be broadcast on DAZN in the UK and various local providers in Australia.

Do not miss this fight. It is a grudge match with a championship on the line, and the atmosphere in Sydney is going to be unforgettable. Whether you love du Plessis's violence or Strickland's precision, this is a fight that will define the middleweight division for years to come.
